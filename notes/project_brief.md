## Title

Alternative titles (German, kept for reference):
- Das Paradox des deutschen Arbeitsmarkts — und die Arbeit von morgen
- Arbeitslos trotz Fachkräftemangel: Was heute nicht passt und morgen gebraucht wird

## Introduction

My task was to analyse why unemployment and skills shortages exist at the same time in Germany. While working with the data, I realised this question can't be separated from another one: which occupations and skills will be needed tomorrow, shaped by AI, automation and demography. So I combined both topics. Today's paradox shows where the gap already exists; the look ahead shows where it is moving.
